How prices are determined

The total price of the translation varies on the number of words, the structure, and how long it takes me to translate it.

Similar documents might be eligible for discounts.


Cost of the page

The price of each page depends on its length and complexity.

Short, simple pages start at 5$.

Very complex pages might cost up to 80$.


Long documents

Longer documents, such as books or contracts, are usually priced per word or per page, with clear estimates provided before we start.


Urgent & bulk work

For urgent requests and large volumes, I always present at least two options, to help you balance speed and cost.

Step 1 - Digitize the documents

Scan the documents or take a clear photo of them.

Make sure all the text is legible, and the pages are not cut off or overlapping.

Hard-to-read documents are ok for the quote, but not for the translation process.

I do not need the originals.


Step 2 - Send me the documents

Send me the documents by email, WhatsApp, or filling the contact form below.

You will receive an answer with a free exact quote of the cost and time needed for the translation, and payment instructions.
